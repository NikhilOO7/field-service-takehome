- Confirm the serial and cover cast mark. Treat any Version 2 cover as affected.
- Clean the casing and cover gasket seats. With the gasket removed, inspect for cracks, preferably with dye penetrant. Replace a cracked cover.
- Fit a new Version 2 gasket: 0.8 mm aramid fibre, graphite faced. Never reuse a gasket or fit a 1.5 mm Version 1 gasket on this seat.
- Lightly oil the bolt threads (property class 8.8). Do not use anti-seize paste with a lower friction value.
- Use a calibrated torque wrench, cross pattern, two passes: first approximately 20 N-m, then 38 N-m. No impact tools.
- After the first 24 hours at operating temperature, check torque once at 38 N-m. Do not exceed this to stop a leak; leakage indicates a damaged gasket or seat.
- Record the applied torque and “SB-2025-009” in the maintenance record.
- This unit previously developed a cracked gasket seat after tightening to 45 N-m (case 00041988). On June 11, 2026, the casing half and gasket were replaced; after 24 hours, joint and seal were dry at discharge 5.6 bar.

> RTM-H-0007 has an L-03 alarm again, suction at 1.9 bar. What should I do?

- Confirm the nameplate serial and review controller alarm history: circuit, time, suction and discharge pressures. Record the applicable campaign and previous cases in the work order.
