My own cheat sheet for E-31 on the VRS-110s, copied from HV-PROC-07 so I don't have to open SharePoint on my phone in the compressor room. Short version only. Not a controlled document.

HV-certified only. If you're not HV, close this page.

E-31 reset, short version

  1. Read the log, note what was happening before the trip. Photo of the screen.
  2. Stop the unit, isolate and lock out per HV-PROC-02.
  3. Wait 5 min for the DC bus to discharge. Don't skip this.
  4. Open the cabinet. Measure DC at TP1/TP2 (bottom left of the drive board, red and black test points). Must be below 50 V DC before you touch anything.
  5. Insulation test on the motor leads, 1,000 V. Need above 100 MOhm. If it was just a grid dip and the unit has been fine for months I usually skip this, it takes forever to get the leads off.
  6. Close the cabinet, remove locks, power up.
  7. Set P9.01 = 1 in the drive menu (fault reset enable), then hold RESET on the drive keypad for 3 s. The panel RESET doesn't work for E-31, it has to be the drive keypad.
  8. P9.01 goes back to 0 by itself after the reset.
  9. Start unloaded and let it run 10 min. Motor current should stay below 210 A. If it goes higher, stop.
  10. If it trips again within 24 h, don't reset again, call Vastra L2.

Takes about 40 min with the insulation test, 20 without.

Notes

  • TP1/TP2 are under the plastic cover on the drive board, you need the long probes.
  • Some of the drives show P9.01 as "Rst En" instead of the number, same thing.
  • Write the readings in the case, Kenji checks.
