Field Service : HCP-500 Maintenance Quick Reference

Created by Tom Becker, last modified on Apr 26, 2023

My notes from the Halden HCP-500 online training (March 2023), tidied up. The first 500 in our fleet goes in at Tacoma in July (TAC-P-0044) and we're helping with commissioning, so I wanted the PM tasks somewhere everyone can find them. This is the short version. The manual (HDN-IOM-500) has the details.

The HCP-500 is a split-case pump, not end-suction like the 350. Two bearing housings, two mechanical seals, top half of the casing lifts off. Don't plan a seal job on a 500 like a 350 job, it's twice the work.

PM intervals

Task Interval Notes
Grease bearings (pump and motor) 2,000 h 25 g per bearing, lithium complex NLGI 2. With the pump running, relief plug open. Don't overdo it
Clean suction strainer Every 500 h Or when the strainer dP alarm comes up. New cover gasket each time (18-1121). Basket is 18-1120
Vibration readings, both bearings 1,000 h Write them in MaintPro so we can trend them
Inspect mechanical seals for leakage 4,000 h Both sides. Kit is 18-0502, you need two
Check coupling alignment 4,000 h or 12 months Laser kit from Fresno
Measure wear ring clearance 8,000 h Limit in the manual, section 8.5
Check foundation bolt torque 8,000 h
Calibrate PT-1 / PT-2 24 months Transmitters are 19-0301

Strainer

The Halden trainer said new installations load the strainer a lot faster in the first weeks because of debris left in new pipework, welding beads, gasket bits and so on. For TAC-P-0044, check the strainer dP daily for the first two weeks and clean as needed, then go to the normal interval.

Cleaning: stop and lock out the pump, close both valves, drain the strainer through its drain, take the cover off, lift out the basket, clean with water and a soft brush, check the mesh, refit with a new cover gasket, open the valves, vent, restart. Check the dP has dropped back.

Vibration

Under 2.8 mm/s is good. 2.8 to 7.1 is OK but trend it. Over 7.1 the controller will alarm (P-03) and you need to do something.

Things the trainer stressed

  • Suction isolation valve fully open in operation. No throttling on the suction side, ever.
  • Never run against a closed discharge valve for more than 30 seconds.
  • Casing bolts in a cross pattern, two passes. Torque is in the manual. Don't overtighten, the casing gasket seat can crack.
  • O-rings on the seals: water or glycerine only.
