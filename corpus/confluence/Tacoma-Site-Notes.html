Field Service : Tacoma Site Notes

Created by Daniel Reyes, last modified on Apr 14, 2026

Stuff I wish someone had told me my first week at TAC. Not official. If the site rules change, the site rules win, and please update this page. - Daniel

Getting in

  • Where: Puget Cold Chain, Tacoma Cold Storage, out in the port area. Do NOT use the main truck gate. Contractors go in at Gate 2 on the frontage road, the one with the yellow barrier.
  • Badge: contractor badge from the security office. You need photo ID and your Corvane ID, plus the site safety video (about 20 minutes) every 12 months. Your badge expires with your video. Dave's team can't override it, so don't ask them at 6 a.m.
  • Hours: security office is staffed 6:00 to 18:00 weekdays. Outside that, call the night supervisor through the gatehouse and someone walks you in. Allow 20 minutes.
  • Parking: contractor lot behind Building C. Not on the dock aprons and not in the fire lane next to the compressor room door. They tow, and they mean it. Ask Priya.

Dave is good to work with but he needs warning. Anything that takes a compressor offline needs a window, and he books those around inbound trucks. Monday and Thursday mornings are the big inbounds, forget those. Ask at least a week ahead and confirm the day before. If he says a window doesn't work, it doesn't work, there's no point pushing.

Hazards (actually read this bit)

Ammonia. The site's main refrigeration plant runs on ammonia. It's the customer's, not ours, we don't touch it. But if the ammonia alarm goes (horn plus amber beacons), you leave. Muster point is the east lot by the wind sock. Look at the sock and go upwind. Escape masks are in the red box at the compressor room door. If you smell ammonia, get out first and call after.

  • Forklifts and reach trucks. Everywhere, all the time, and they have right of way. Walk in the green lanes. High-vis on, even in the corridor to the compressor room. The corners at the dock end are blind and the drivers are quick.
  • Cold store. The freezer rooms run at about -10 F (-23 C). If you have to go in (you mostly won't, our stuff is outside them), take a freezer jacket from the rack by the door, tell someone, and come out after 45 minutes max to warm up. There's a push bar on the inside of the door, check it works before the door closes behind you.
  • Ice. The floor at the dock doors is ice in winter. Boots with grip.
  • Noise. Compressor room B with all three units loaded is loud. Plugs in.
  • Roof. We don't do roof work at TAC. The roof units are the site's own contractor's.

About 0142: it's wedged in the back corner and you need to move the oil drums to get round the cooler side. 0142 sits right next to the freezer exhaust; the TS-2 harness runs along the duct. In summer it's like an oven back there, bring water and don't lean on the duct, it's hot. The cooler on 0142 also gets dirtier than on 0143 for the same reason, so give it extra time on PMs.

Compressor room B in general: no wifi and the LTE drops to one bar or nothing. Download manuals, logs and firmware files before you go in. The ServiceTool laptop works offline. The room exhaust fan switch is by the door, it should be on Auto. If someone left it on Off, the whole room cooks.

Practical stuff

  • Ladders and lifting: the site has a step ladder in room B you can borrow, ask Dave's guys first. No lifting gear for airends or motors, that's a rental and a plan.
  • Waste oil and filters: waste oil drum is in the maintenance shop, there's a log sheet on the clipboard, fill it in. Used filters go in the oily waste bin next to it, not in the general trash.
  • X10 firmware cable: there are two in NA. One lives in my van, one is usually in Fresno. If you need it, ask in #na-compressors before you drive out, not after.
  • Coffee: break room in Building A, contractors welcome, put a couple of dollars in the jar. Taco truck at Gate 2 on Tuesdays and Fridays around 11.
  • Restrooms: Building A or the dock office. Not the ones in the cold store corridor, they're for the warehouse crew and they will tell you.
