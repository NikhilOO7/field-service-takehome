Field Service : VRS Series Oil and Filter Quick Reference

Created by Priya Natarajan, last modified on Mar 21, 2024

Quick reference for PM visits on the Vastra VRS compressors. I put this together during my apprentice placement in Tacoma so I didn't have to scroll through three manuals in the compressor room with one bar of signal. Values are from the current Vastra service manuals (March 2024). Kenji checked it.

Print this page and keep it in the van binder. The compressor room at TAC has no signal.

Intervals

Model Oil Oil change Oil filter Air filter Separator element
VRS-45 SynLube 46 or mineral ISO 46 (check the asset) 4,000 h With every oil change (ask stores, not stocked in Tacoma) 2,000 h, 41-1510 8,000 h, 41-3070
VRS-75 SynLube 46 4,000 h With every oil change, 41-1470 2,000 h, 41-1510 8,000 h, 41-3075
VRS-110 SynLube 46 4,000 h With every oil change, 41-1480 2,000 h, 41-1512 8,000 h, 41-3075

All intervals are running hours or 12 months, whichever comes first. Shorten the air filter interval if the unit trips on air filter restriction before the 2,000 h mark (Tacoma, I'm looking at you).

Oil

  • Vastra SynLube 46 comes in 20 L drums, part 41-0046. One drum does a VRS-75 change with some left over. A 110 needs most of a drum.
  • Never mix SynLube with mineral oil. If a unit is on mineral oil, it stays on mineral oil until someone does a full flush and changeover.
  • Check the oil type on the fleet register before you leave the shop.
  • Waste oil goes in the site drum, not in the van.

On site

  1. Run the unit for 10 minutes so the oil is warm and drains better.
  2. Stop, isolate, wait for the separator to depressurise fully. Check the gauge reads zero.
  3. Drain the oil from the separator drain valve into a clean container so you can look at it. Milky oil means water, black and sludgy means it was overdue.
  4. Change the oil filter. Oil the new gasket. Hand tight plus three-quarters of a turn.
  5. Fill to the middle of the sight glass with the unit stopped.
  6. Run, check for leaks, check the level again after 10 minutes stopped.
  7. Reset the service counter on the controller and record the hours in MaintPro.
